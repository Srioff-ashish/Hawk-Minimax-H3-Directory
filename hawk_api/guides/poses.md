For explicit scenes between consenting adults. Both people are always adults; the server refuses anything else.

## Rules for every position

- Models do not know position names. "Full nelson" or "anvil" in a prompt does nothing on its own. Write the geometry: where each body is, what carries its weight, where every limb goes, where the two bodies touch, and which way each one faces. Keep the name only as a label.
- Name penetration plainly: vaginal or anal, and the direction he enters from (from behind, from above, from below). Every entry below works for both unless it says otherwise; for anal, say so in the same sentence as the position, not in a later one.
- Two complete, separate bodies. Give each body its own sentences and account for all four of its limbs ("her left leg ... her right leg ..."). Otherwise the engines merge the bodies, drop a leg or add a spare arm. Say whose hand is where: "his right hand", never just "a hand".
- Weight and support: say what holds each body up (the bed under her back, his knees, his arms, the wall). A body with nothing named under it floats or slides.
- Faces: say where each face points and what it shows (eyes closed, mouth open, looking back over her shoulder, looking down at her). In rear-entry and standing positions her face is easily lost; turn it toward the camera or a mirror if it matters.
- Pick the camera angle the entry tells you to. Side profile shows the contact in the lying positions. A three-quarter rear view works for rear entry, and a low angle from the foot of the bed works for her on top. A camera on the wrong side hides the pose and the model guesses. Say the framing too: full figure for standing positions, hip-to-head for lying ones.
- Video: one position per segment. Changing position inside a segment breaks the bodies; a change of position is a new segment. Describe the motion that position makes (whose hips move, the rhythm, what sways or grips) and keep it possible: the body that carries the weight cannot also be the one swinging freely. Name the speed (slow, steady, fast) and keep it the same for the whole segment unless the change is the point.
- Sound (video): the slap, breath and moans follow the rhythm you wrote; script them in the soundscape like any other sound, with the exclusion sentence.
- With picture references, say which <Picture N> is which person before describing the pose, and keep each person's face, skin and build tied to their own reference. A pose reference (<Pose N>) shows the bodies' positions only; still describe them in words.
- Not every item in a sex manual can be shown. A pelvic-floor squeeze (pompoir) happens inside the body: show its effect on faces and breath, not as a position. A full-body rotation without withdrawing (the classical "acrobatic turn") cannot be shown in one shot either: cut between two positions instead.
