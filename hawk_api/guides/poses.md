## Rules for every position

- Write the geometry and the tags. The base models know no position names: write where each body is, what carries its weight, where every limb goes, where the bodies touch and which way each faces. - Name penetration plainly: vaginal or anal, and the direction he enters from (from behind, from above, from below). Every entry below works for both unless it says otherwise; for anal, say so in the same sentence as the position, not in a later one.

- Weight and support: say what holds each body up (the bed under her back, his knees, his arms, the wall). A body with nothing named under it floats or slides.
- Faces: say where each face points and what it shows (eyes closed, mouth open, looking back over her shoulder, looking down at her). In rear-entry and standing positions her face is easily lost; turn it toward the camera or a mirror if it matters.

When a thigh, hip or his back would be between the camera and the point where they join, move the camera rather than hope.

Never write "two complete bodies" or describe his whole body in a POV prompt: that pulls the second body back in, and Qwen Image 2.1 merges or breaks it. - Kneeling: write "her knees on the bed on either side of his waist, her shins flat on the bed behind her", not just "kneels astride". Krea 2 follows it. - Pick the camera angle the entry tells you to. Side profile shows the contact in the lying positions. Say the framing too: full figure for standing positions, hip-to-head for lying ones.

Describe the motion that position makes (whose hips move, the rhythm, what sways or grips) and keep it possible: the body that carries the weight cannot also be the one swinging freely. Name the speed (slow, steady, fast) and keep it the same for the whole segment unless the change is the point.
